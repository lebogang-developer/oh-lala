About OH LALA

Bringing People Together Through Great Food & Good Times

More than just a restaurant, OH LALA Pub & Grill is where family, friends and communities come together to create unforgettable memories.

Our Story

Built Around Food, Family & Celebration

OH LALA Pub & Grill was created with one vision: to provide a welcoming space where people can enjoy delicious meals, refreshing drinks and memorable celebrations.

Over the years, we've become a favourite destination in Thaba Nchu for birthdays, baby showers, family gatherings, corporate functions and casual dining.

Every meal, every event and every guest matters to us.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional dining experiences, quality food and memorable events that bring people together.

Our Vision

To become the most loved restaurant, pub and event venue in the Free State.
